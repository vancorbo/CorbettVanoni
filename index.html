About

Artist, cartoonist
& illustrator.

Corbett Vanoni is an illustrator, cartoonist, and fine artist based in Minneapolis, Minnesota. Born and raised in Southern California, he spent many years working as an art director and prepress technician while also freelancing in animation and illustration. In 2008, Corbett moved from Los Angeles to Minneapolis, where he now works full time from his studio in the historic California Building, a former grain mill turned art space. (Serendipity?) Since 2013, Corbett has been the house animator and illustrator for the educational music group The Learning Station. From 2014 until its closure in 2025, he also created many of the illustrations and images used by Minneapolis restaurant Market Bar-B-Que. Looking ahead, Corbett is focusing increasingly on his work as a panel cartoonist and fine artist.
